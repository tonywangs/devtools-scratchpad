Simulation contract · five fields · gaps skipped · folds counted twice

Numeric minute, hour, day-of-month, month and day-of-week. Wildcards, lists, ascending ranges and steps on wildcards/ranges only. Sunday is 0 or 7. Day fields use AND if either starts with *, OR otherwise. No names, macros, seconds, L/W/#/? or hashed extensions.

Nonexistent local minutes produce no instant; repeated local minutes may produce two. The UTC interval includes its start and excludes its end. Matching does not guarantee execution; daemons and hosted schedulers may behave differently. Commands are never executed.

Limits: 24 schedules · 366 days · 10,000 occurrences · 8 seconds per evaluation · years 2000–2100. An empty interval result is not proof of impossibility. Truncated or interrupted runs remain incomplete.
